# Visual Contract — Odd Shift Duo

## Identity anchors

- **Chaos (left):** compact, broad silhouette; bald head; short dark stubble; blue hoodie under a black jacket; oversized black headphones around the neck; puckered comic expression.
- **Deadpan (right):** taller, slim silhouette; swept brown hair; white short-sleeve polo with a fictional gold crescent pin; asymmetrical raised brow and crooked half-smile.
- The two must read as a pair even at thumbnail size through height, mass, color, and expression contrast.

## Style

- Original stylized 3D characters with semi-real materials and deliberately exaggerated facial acting.
- Rounded, game-readable forms with believable clothing layers and small prop details.
- No photo texture projection and no premade character mesh.
- Blue and gold rim lighting reinforces the clothing color split.

## Animation contract

- One synchronized skeletal rig containing both characters.
- Four clearly separated beats: curious idle, swagger walk, high-five, and victory pose.
- Motion must show contrasting personalities: Chaos overcommits; Deadpan reacts late and economically.
- Rooted feet and readable silhouettes take priority over motion complexity.

## Game-delivery contract

- Editable Blender source.
- Binary glTF export with meshes, materials, armature, and animation.
- Animation frame manifest for easy clip splitting in an engine.
- Rendered poster and H.264 preview video.
- Reference photograph is not embedded in the exported asset.

## Acceptance checks

- Both characters are recognizable from wardrobe, head/hair, and expression cues.
- Exactly two character silhouettes and one shared rig export.
- GLB contains meshes, skins/joints, and at least one animation.
- Representative frames show idle, high-five, and victory without obvious intersections.
- Preview and source open successfully.
